Wardrobe & Prep

Keep it Comfortable

Choose pieces you can move in—flowy dresses, linen, soft tees. Avoid heavy logos.

Coordinate, Don’t Match

Pick 2–3 colors (neutrals + one accent). Mix textures for depth.

Bring the Little Things

Water, wipes, hair ties, a favorite toy or blanket for kids—tiny items, big help.

FAQ

What happens if it rains?

We watch the radar together. If weather looks rough, we reschedule quickly to your next available golden-hour.

How many images will we receive?

Minis average 25–40 selects; full sessions 60–100+. You’ll receive a private, downloadable gallery.

Can we include extended family?

Yes! We’ll plan groupings ahead of time so the flow stays easy. A small add-on covers the extra time.

Ready to make something you’ll be proud to share?

Tell me who’s coming, where you love, and the vibe you’re after—I’ll take care of the rest.
