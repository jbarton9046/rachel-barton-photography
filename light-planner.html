What are “golden” and “blue” hours?

Golden hour is when the sun is low and light is soft and warm. On most days it’s roughly the first hour after sunrise and the last hour before sunset.

Blue hour hugs the edges of daylight—when the sun is just below the horizon and the sky turns deep blue. It usually occurs right before sunrise and right after sunset, during civil twilight.

Sunrise / Sunset Golden hour Blue hour

Golden hour is approximated by sun elevations from −4° to +6°. Blue hour aligns to civil twilight (−6° to −4°). Values handle daylight saving automatically.
